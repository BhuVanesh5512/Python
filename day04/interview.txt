7.Explain this pipeline:

      ↓

      ↓
lower()
      ↓
replace()
      ↓
split()    Why might an AI application perform these transformations before processing text?

my answer : to normalize text and make it list

professional answer :
RAW USER INPUT
      ↓
    strip()
      ↓
 remove unnecessary surrounding whitespace
      ↓
    lower()
      ↓
 normalize letter case
      ↓
   replace()
      ↓
 remove/change unwanted patterns
      ↓
    split()
      ↓
 convert text into individual tokens/words
 An AI application may preprocess raw text to clean and normalize inconsistent input before downstream processing. 
 strip() removes unwanted surrounding whitespace, lower() can normalize case, replace() can remove or transform specific patterns, and split() can convert text into a list of words/tokens when word-level processing is needed. 
 The exact preprocessing depends on the application because removing information unnecessarily can also be harmful.
 #Real AI systems do NOT blindly apply all of these transformations.
 #Preprocessing should be driven by the task, not applied automatically.
