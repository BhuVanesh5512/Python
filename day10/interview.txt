Which relationship is inheritance, and which is composition? Explain why they should not be reversed.
my answer :

profession answer :
Explain why they should not be reversed.
They should not be reversed because these relationships express different things. A text model is a kind of AI model; 
an application has or uses a model. Composition also lets you replace the model without redesigning the application.


'''
Inheritance models an “is-a” relationship. Composition models a “has-a” or “uses-a” relationship. 
Composition makes an application flexible because it depends on an object's expected behavior rather than requiring its own logic to be rewritten for every model.
'''
